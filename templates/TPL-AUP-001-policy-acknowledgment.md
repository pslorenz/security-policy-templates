## How to use this template

POL-GOV-001 Section 3.8 requires everyone to acknowledge POL-AUP-001 and POL-GOV-001 before they receive access, and again every year. This template has two parts: the form each person signs (Part 1), and the log that tracks who has signed and when (Part 2).

Auditors and cyber insurance carriers usually ask for the log first, then for a sample of signed forms. Keep both.

**Who signs.** Employees, contractors, interns, temporary staff, and anyone else who receives an account on [ORGANIZATION_NAME] systems. Vendors whose staff use [ORGANIZATION_NAME] accounts sign for each named individual, or their contract includes equivalent terms under POL-TPRM-001.

**When.** Before access is granted, every year by [ANNUAL_ACKNOWLEDGMENT_MONTH, e.g., January], and within thirty days after a material change to either policy.

**How.** Electronic signature through the HR system, a document signing service, or a form that records the person's name and the date is acceptable, as long as the record shows who acknowledged which version and when. Paper forms are scanned and filed.

**Not a confidentiality agreement.** The form confirms that the person has read the policies and understands their obligations, including confidentiality. It does not replace the confidentiality agreement required by POL-PPS-001 Section 3.7, which has legal weight this form is not written to carry. Many organizations sign both on the same day.

## Part 1: Acknowledgment Form

**[ORGANIZATION_NAME] Security Policy Acknowledgment**

I acknowledge that:

1. I have received and read the Acceptable Use Policy (POL-AUP-001, version [AUP_VERSION]) and the Information Security Policy (POL-GOV-001, version [GOV_VERSION]), and I know where to find them.

2. I understand that I am responsible for following these policies when I use [ORGANIZATION_NAME] systems, devices, data, and accounts, wherever I am working.

3. I understand that [ORGANIZATION_NAME] monitors the use of its systems for security and operational purposes, as described in POL-AUP-001 Section 3.1, and that I should not expect privacy in my use of company systems beyond what law provides.

4. I will protect [ORGANIZATION_NAME] information according to its classification, and I will use only approved tools, including approved AI tools, to handle company data.

5. I will keep my account credentials to myself, use multi-factor authentication, and never share a password or approve a sign-in request I did not start.

6. I will report suspected security incidents, phishing, lost or stolen devices, and data sent to the wrong person promptly, using [REPORTING_CHANNEL]. I understand that reporting a mistake promptly is always the right thing to do.

7. I understand that my obligation to protect confidential information continues after my work with [ORGANIZATION_NAME] ends, and that I must return company devices and information when it ends.

8. I understand that violating these policies may result in loss of access and disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.

## Part 2: Acknowledgment Log

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | | | | | | |

**Checks each quarter:**

No one has an access granted date earlier than their initial acknowledgment date. Anyone who does is a finding, because POL-GOV-001 Section 3.8 requires acknowledgment before access.

No one is more than thirty days past their annual due date. Overdue acknowledgments are escalated to the person's manager, and access may be suspended under POL-GOV-001 until the acknowledgment is complete.

After a material policy change, everyone has acknowledged the new version within thirty days.
